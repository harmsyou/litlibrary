# Research Canvas

i want to build a personal website where i can upload research papers, highlight text, comment on the text, and take notes on the paper parallel-ly, and then the work (information i write down while reading the paper and the comment on specific text both go to a main doc i have that is like my centralized understanding of that concept with my notes on the topic, and quotes and comments from all papers I route to that topic...so for example, maybe i upload a pdf research paper and as i am reading it, on the side, i have different note dropdown options (maybe on learning, memory, molecular organization, idk) and i can click one to markdown and take notes on the research paper's resulting information as I relate it to one of those topics and then for whatever dropdown i have open, any comments i make from certain highlights of the text, it also appears in my centralized notes of the topic (that dropdown). the comments of the paper also should be shown kind of like a google doc where when i open that paper, i can see the little callout comments and highlights. so my personal research website here should have a landing page with acess to all of my centralized notes on whatever topics, as well as a database of all of the research papers i upload and i can click into either (the centralized note page of something or a paper) individually or bring a paper up and then parallel-ly access the notes. something to keep in mind though is that the notes i have access to when commenting on the paper and writing my information while reading the paper on the side (notes on the right, research paper on the left) should be not centralized and only my notes for the paper (and the comments are only in callout form near the paper (on the left side), and then i only see the centralized version of notes from all related papers and comments on the centralized notes page...let me know if you need clarification before starting to build.

i want a sans serif UI...maybe inspire the UI based off of design inspiration from the designer "and-now"? or "works in progress"? even though i know they are heavily serif based...otherwise just a clean research-first website
